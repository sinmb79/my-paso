# Third-Party Data Notices

## Wikidata POI seed

Hello! My Paso! bundles a 2026-07-19 snapshot of 100 South Korean place records derived from Wikidata structured data.

- Source: [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/)
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- Wikidata policy: [Wikidata:Licensing](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing)
- Query endpoint: [Wikidata Query Service](https://query.wikidata.org/sparql)

The bundled fields are entity IDs, labels, instance types, coordinates, administrative labels, and sitelink counts used only for deterministic selection. No Wikipedia prose or Wikimedia Commons media is bundled. Record-level entity URLs, queries, selection rules, and the released seed hash are preserved in `src/lib/poi/seed-manifest.json`.

CC0 does not require attribution. This notice is included for provenance and user transparency.
